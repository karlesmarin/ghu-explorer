🧪 A research laboratory for gauge‑Higgs unification

Pick a model of extra‑dimensional matter and this page computes what it predicts: which Wilson‑line domain is legal, where the vacuum sits, the Higgs mass, the anomaly bill, and how high the compactification scale can go. Its interactive calculations and reference examples run offline in your browser. Optional PhaseTracer and HiggsTools calculations use a local scientific engine — and every result states its assumptions.

I read the paper and want to try it → The instrument. Twenty-nine menu sections, three Simulator model modes and eight embedded experiment cards, with integrated diagnostics and experimental comparisons. I found the tool and want to know what it is → The series. Nine parts on gauge‑Higgs unification in ten records, each with its record, its status, and what has moved since.
Read this before quoting a number from here. Our α does not reproduce the published α of N. Haba, T. Yamashita, JHEP 02 (2004) 059 (hep-ph/0401185), eq. (3.20), and the disagreement is not a constant — so it is not a convention that could be absorbed. Every absolute scale on this site (TeV, GeV) inherits that open question and is not citable until it closes. What escapes it entirely, because no normalisation enters them: the mass ratio, the bill in eighths, and the two arithmetic laws. The instrument states this in its own header and carries a permanent “what this cannot tell you” panel; the reasoning is in the log.

🧾 Every number carries what is known about it

Four words, and they are fields in the exported result card rather than decoration. This is the whole difference between an instrument and a demonstration.

wordwhat it promises
theorem Proved, in a paper of the series, and the page names which one.
verified Checked against an independent computation — usually brute force over the same object — but not proved.
measured Computed here, and inheriting whatever open questions its inputs carry. Every absolute scale on this site is of this kind.
unknown Said out loud, with the reason, instead of a plausible number. The tool refuses to fill a gap it cannot compute.

The fourth is the one that costs something, so it is worth saying why it is there. This project has retracted one of its own novelty claims before — a closed‑form identity published in May 2026 and withdrawn in July on finding it was a corollary of Helset–Martin–Trott 2020. The algebra was right; the claim about its standing was not. A tool that could not label its own output unknown would have no way to do that to itself.

📝 What has moved

The papers are frozen; this site is not. Every change is logged with what moved, why, and whether it affects the version of record.

2026-10-07extensionthe instrument · affects the record: no

Integrated transition history, conditional rung bounds and reproducible assumption studies

what

Thermal SU(3) now integrates nucleation, percolation, completion and expanding bubble volumes from refined PhaseTracer actions. A gated acoustic spectrum retains supplied wall, efficiency, expansion and reheating assumptions. Screen a table gains interval certificates for listed candidate and published rungs, plus independent full-Fourier witness checks. Three reproducible studies archive actual HiggsTools coupling/width profiles, 160 thermal scenarios and 80 selected vacuum checks after a budgeted candidate enumeration. Both repository READMEs, panel help, public documentation, result-card guidance and the handoff describe the new routes, outputs, reproduction commands and limits.

why

The S3/T proxy does not itself establish percolation or completion. A small-angle moment ceiling does not certify the full-potential vacuum. A Higgs width constraint depends on which couplings may vary. Each calculation now exposes these distinctions with the corresponding numerical evidence and assumptions.

so

The certificates remain conditional small-angle bounds; selected full-potential survivors are numerical witnesses, not proven global optima. The thermal acoustic amplitude is sensitive to supplied plasma parameters. The scalar fit, thermal SU(3) and candidate SU(7) are separate models; no combined GHU likelihood or detector significance is claimed. Existing papers and archived editions are unchanged.

2026-10-07extensionthe instrument · affects the record: no

Fixed light inputs, neutrino responses and descriptive experimental-reference figures

what

A new experiment reconstructs the same light inputs along nine ring/deficit paths. Five descriptive figures display heavy centres, splitting, raw and explicitly normalized vacuum current factors, a magnified difference and the archived DeepCore 2018 reference map. The fourth study category archives 738 scan points and standalone SVG/PNG/PDF figures. Original experimental tables, hashes, independent NumPy/SVD references and browser checks accompany the code. Both READMEs, inventory, help and public guides describe the complete route.

why

Reproducing supplied masses does not make them predictions. A research tool must show which additional responses change, where normalization hides an effect, and which data assumptions permit a comparison. Captions, scales, fixed-input labels and difference plots make this visible.

so

This is tree-level, leading-Majorana reconstruction with specified vacuum current normalization. DeepCore is an archived standard-three-neutrino reference, not a nonunitary ring fit. No ordering odds, extrapolation, ring exclusion or sum with NuFIT is assigned. Detector response, matter, weak-input refits and radiative stability remain open. Frozen papers and editions are unchanged.

🔧 The code

The instrument is one self-contained HTML file with no automatic external requests, built from a Python and JavaScript source tree by build/build_app.py, which re-runs every harness and refuses to publish a red build. It is a living instrument and it is rewritten often; what travels with a paper is not this tool but its evidence — the gate scripts and their archived receipts, from which every number in that paper regenerates without this site. What we do keep alive is every URL a published record points at — see editions — and the log of what has moved since.
