Part VII · An Upper Bound on the Compactification Scale of SU(7) Grand Gauge-Higgs Unification, and the Dijet Angular Distribution That Tests It

version of record10.5281/zenodo.22087251 · v1, 2026-08-24 · frozen, and it stays frozen

Abstract

In gauge-Higgs unification the Higgs is the Wilson-line phase, so the electroweak hierarchy is a minimum of a potential rather than a ratio of scales. Part VI computed the curvature of that vacuum for the SU(7) model of Komori and Maru (arXiv:2503.04090) and reached a sign. This paper computes the vacuum itself — and then asks how large the compactification scale can be over arbitrary multiplicities of the representations they put in the bulk. The answer is finite, and a collider recast of CMS’s dijet angular distribution approaches the same scale from below, so the two calculations cross.

One ladder, three rungs. Expanding the one-loop effective potential about the symmetric point produces a single identity read at p = 5, 3, 1: the value, the curvature and the fourth moment are the same formula at three values of one index. At p = 1 the antiperiodic halves cancel their pole into a finite ln 2 while the periodic pole survives as the x4ln x branch point — which is why the answer cannot be a polynomial, where the finite-temperature theory at an even index has Gross–Pisarski–Yaffe’s exact quartic.

The hierarchy in closed form. Solving stationarity gives αmin in a Lambert-W form in three moments, x² = −d/W(−de−b) — not an algebraic root, since it carries ln x, but solved rather than iterated, with the two branches the minimum and the maximum and the fold at zero Higgs mass. Median error 0.13 % over 272 contents against numerical minimisation of the same potential. The logarithm and the third moment cancel identically at the stationary point, so both columns of their Table 1 become functions of two moments with no minimisation anywhere.

The arithmetic of the content. The moments are quantised: 8D ≡ 2A4 + 3 (mod 6), because c4 ≡ c² (mod 3). What holds whatever the gauge sector contributes is the matter half — every bulk multiplet moves 8D − 2A4 by a multiple of six — while the residue belongs to the gauge base point. Whether the same argument also makes 8D odd is a separate question, and it is the one open input of this paper.

The ceiling. Maximising the compactification scale is an integer program over a rational cone, closed by an exact two-variable dual whose vertices are enumerated in rational arithmetic: 1/R5 ≤ 10.03 TeV for arbitrary bulk content. Writing the third moment in the basis {1, ln 2, ln 3} lifts the content to a four-dimensional lattice, where the relaxation’s vertex turns out empty and the bound sharpens to 10.01 TeV attained. Put on the exact potential, however, that content is a false vacuum. Requiring the electroweak point to be the true minimum is the orbifold stability criterion of Cacciapaglia, Cossu and Deandrea, whose arithmetic is theirs; summed over a content it is a fourth linear functional, and what is new is only that a linear functional fits inside the integer program. With it imposed the bound is 9.22 TeV on the three-rung algebraic surface, and 9.09 TeV at the Higgs mass the world actually has.

Five coordinates, and they are complete. Two bulk contents have the same one-loop potential, as a function of the phase, if and only if they agree on five integer invariants. That is proved by two kernels meeting, computed by routes sharing no arithmetic, and it explains a gap the earlier work had left open between a certificate and an enumeration.

Which state a collider would bound. Read off their three parity matrices and nothing else: every colour-changing gauge boson is odd under the small-circle parity, so its tower starts at 1/R6 and its wavefunction vanishes at the fixed point where that same paper localises the quarks — which answers, for the gauge channel, the rapid proton decay their conclusions flag and leave to future work. What survives at the brane acts through a colour-octet operator in the dijet angular distribution, so published contact-interaction limits, stated for a colour-singlet operator, do not directly apply to it; the data behind them do constrain it, which is what the recast does. Carried through CMS’s unfolded distribution, 77×77 covariance and NNLO baseline gives a nominal sensitivity of 1/R5 > 10.86 TeV. It is deliberately not called an exclusion: a Gaussian χ² over unfolded data is not a detector-level CLs, and the paper measures that gap rather than waving at it — aimed at their own published limit the machinery returns 17.5 against 17 TeV and 29.5 against 37.

The one condition, and it is not hidden. The chain passes through one coefficient this paper does not derive from the product-orbifold geometry: the gauge weight of eq. (68) of arXiv:2503.04090. Theorem 1 carries its hypothesis in its own statement, and a red note on the front page says so. A parity-resolved covariant count of the same orbit, done here, gives a different split — shown to be independent of the gauge parameter, with the ghosts’ boundary conditions derived from the orbifold action on the gauge parameter rather than assumed. So the fork is not symmetric, and the paper says so: its own machinery points at the branch where that hypothesis is not met. Both branches are costed in a table, and no number is quoted without saying which branch it lives on. Between the two seeds considered, and on the three-rung surface, 9.22 TeV remains the conservative bound: the ambiguity does not open a route to larger scales, it closes one.

Reproducibility. Every displayed number regenerates from the ancillary scripts, whose standard output is archived beside them; the ledger cites the script for each entry rather than the number alone, so a reader who disagrees with a claim can re-run the thing that made it. Fifteen gates guard the two editions, and one of them executes every cited script and diffs it against its archive: 70 of 70 executable scripts reproduce byte for byte. Two claims the earlier drafts made are corrected in place and left visible rather than removed.

Changes since the record

2026-08-26noteaffects the record: no

The instrument stood on the draft; it now stands on the published paper

what

The hierarchy and anomalies sections were closed on the 9 August draft of Part VII, whose ceiling was the relaxation's 10.03 TeV, called certified and drawn as one point. The paper as deposited on 24 August keeps that number as the bound the dual proves and adds three levels below it: 10.01 TeV attained at (A₄, 8D) = (212, 1) once the lattice is lifted — the relaxation's own vertex (215, 1) is empty — 9.22 TeV once the electroweak point must be the true vacuum, and 9.09 TeV at the Higgs mass the Higgs actually has. The content that attains 10.01 sits in a false vacuum: the potential is deeper at the other symmetric point by 316. The instrument had no way to see that, because it did not compute W.

It now does. Every content carries W = Σ_{c odd} m(−s), F(1) − F(0) = (31/16) ζ(5) W exactly, and a verdict — the numbers at a stationary point in a false vacuum are still shown, labelled. The ceiling a content is measured against is 9.22 TeV, and the four levels are a table with what each one bounds. Theorem 1 (8D odd) is now stated as the paper states it, conditional on the gauge seed: a switch lets the model stand on the candidate parity-resolved split of §13, under which 8D is even, A₄ half-integral, the relaxation ceiling drops to 7.38 TeV at 8D = 2, and Theorem 2 and the 2W theorem survive untouched. The five complete invariants (A₄, 8D, 2U, V, 2W) are computed in the kernel and checked, generator by generator, against lattice_lift.py.

why

A page that says "certified ceiling 10.03 TeV" over a content in a false vacuum is showing a number the paper it advertises has since qualified. Publishing a part did not update the instrument built on its draft — the same failure that left the series pages saying "not yet deposited" for five days after Part VI was deposited, and it was found by auditing the whole site against the papers rather than the part that had just moved.

so

The record is unaffected: nothing in the frozen Part VII depends on this page. Two things in the instrument were wrong and are corrected — the single ceiling, and Theorem 1 stated without its hypothesis — and one thing was missing, the stability functional, which is [8]'s criterion and not ours. And one bug of the shell surfaced while photographing the seed switch: a page opened with a permalink rendered nothing at all, so every deep link the tool had ever handed out was dead. Fixed in the same commit; the harness now opens one.
