Boundary research: spectra, profiles, anomalies and certified response

Six separate tests help investigate a proposed GHU boundary mechanism: roots of a flat fermion boundary problem, the numerical rank of overlapping profiles, exact parity-inserted spinor traces and a restricted six-dimensional SU(2) tensor condition. The fifth compares two wall profiles after common pole and residue matching, using replayed continuum certificates. The sixth counts finite neutral-complex classes on exact strata.

What this tool is for

Use these tools to find which requirement fails and why before attempting a full model. A mass gap addresses unwanted paired modes; Gram rank tests linear independence; inserted traces expose localized anomaly terms; the tensor condition tests a restricted quantization requirement. Passing these tests does not prove a viable Standard Model embedding or a complete quantum theory. Matched response asks whether fixing two local conditions removes all finite-width profile dependence in the declared quadratic model.

First steps

  1. Open Anomalies & proton and find Boundary research. Select English in the card. Press Research baseline to restore all inputs; Demo runs the worked examples and ends with this guide.
  2. Set Boundary strength to 0, then 1. Compare the first normal root and the paired curve at the same imposed Wilson angle.
  3. Set Profile example M to 0, then 1. Compare the Gram eigenvalues and numerical rank.
  4. Switch the parity insertion between C1, C0 and C0 W. Read the exact trace table, keeping its degree and support dimension in mind.
  5. With lepton chirality Opposite to Q, compare one and three identical 6D families. Read the signed doublet excess and its residue.
  6. Select Certified response query: t = 1/4, then t = 1/2. Read section 5 and the positive lower bound of uniform minus triangular. Download the proof and certificate ZIP and follow its README to replay the independent Sage verification.
  7. Select Exact neutral stratum: s ≠ 0 and s = 0. In section 6 the quotient dimension changes from 0 to 1. Download the ZIP and run python verify.py certificate.json with SymPy.
  8. Expand assumptions and provenance. Pin a comparison, export the card JSON and save the application link to repeat the calculation.

Reading the results

Read each result with its own assumptions. The normal gap is dimensionless kR; a Gram rank is numerical and threshold-dependent; the traces are exact scalar evaluations without local weights; a zero residue passes only the stated standalone six-dimensional condition. Full anomaly cancellation stays unknown. In section 5, positive difference bounds establish separation at the two admitted points for this model. They do not establish a complete GHU prediction or a thin-layer rate. In section 6, dim ker A = 5 in both strata; rank M = 5 or 4, and dim(ker A/im M) = 0 or 1. The stabilizer ker M is a different space. The exact selector is independent of the Wilson slider.

Worked example

Find three different reasons a construction can fail

  1. Load Research baseline, then Uncoupled boundary: the first normal root is zero.
  2. Restore boundary strength 1: the first kR is approximately 0.273853.
  3. Load Massless profile example: the three chosen profiles span only two independent directions.
  4. Restore M = 1: the eigenvalues are approximately 0.172999, 2.783424 and 10.686355, and the numerical rank is three.
  5. Select One 6D family with Opposite to Q: excess 2 gives residue 2. Select Three 6D families: excess 6 gives residue 0.
  6. Switch C1 to C0 W and inspect the trace table, then export JSON.
  7. Select each certified response query, download the certificate, then save the shared JSON and link. The selected probe, fixed model and certificate identifier travel with the result.
  8. Compare Exact neutral stratum: s ≠ 0 and s = 0. Observe rank M = 5 and 4, and quotient dimension 0 and 1; download the finite proof and verifier.

What to expect: The boundary gap, profile independence and restricted family condition respond to different changes. None of them settles localized or global anomaly cancellation of the full construction. The quotient jump is a certified finite result; it determines neither continuous modes nor a Higgs mass.

Formulas and conventions

Q(t) = F(t) - F(s) - (t-s) F′(s); C(t) = -1 / [(t-s)/R + Q(t)]

Exact matching identity for F(t)=t/D(t), with the same source and fixed s,R. The certified denominators exclude zero at the selected probes.

Scope and limitations

  • The flat spectrum assumes uniform coupling, no additional boundary kinetic terms and the periodic transverse branch. It does not determine a Wilson vacuum, realistic flavour or neutrino masses.
  • The profile calculation is a separate mathematical example, not a derivation of three physical families. Numerical rank depends on tolerance; exact zero modes need an analytic boundary-domain analysis.
  • Inserted traces omit projector and covering-space normalization, consistent descent, mirror contributions and inflow. The scalar probe is not the full anomaly polynomial.
  • The SU(2) criterion applies to a specified standalone six-dimensional tensor setting. It cannot be transferred automatically to an eight-dimensional orbifold corner coupled to bulk inflow.
  • Local coefficients, integral tensor charge lattice, global gauge group, new boson zero modes and full quantum consistency remain unresolved.
  • Certification is restricted to the declared unit-square domain and two fixed probes at finite layer width. Changing the source, forms, matching conditions or width requires a new certificate. Software tests and hashes alone are not mathematical certification.
  • Section 6 certifies only the specified matrices A,M, not the continuous domain or a Higgs mass. Videos and manuals predating 26 October retain their earlier scope.

If something is unclear

The first normal root is zero.

Check boundary strength. At zero coupling this is the expected zero mode; it is not a numerical solver failure.

Overlapping profiles still have rank three.

Overlap measures nonorthogonality. Dependence requires a null Gram direction; inspect the eigenvalues and threshold.

An inserted moment is zero.

Check its degree and insertion. Evaluate the polynomial or more probes before claiming cancellation; this interface shows only one fixed probe.

Three families pass but full cancellation is unknown.

That is intentional: the modular condition is necessary in its stated setting, not a complete local/global anomaly calculation.

An invalid input leaves the old result visible.

The controls report the error and keep the last valid calculation. Restore the allowed range or press Research baseline.

Why can I not change layer width or import a response?

Only independently certified instances are admitted. New computations belong in the external mathematical engine until their witnesses and functional premises are verified.
