5 · Before you compute anything

Glossary — every symbol on this page, in one place

α = (α₁, α₂)
The two Wilson-line phases. In gauge–Higgs unification the Higgs is this phase, so the potential over α is the Higgs potential, and its minimum is the vacuum.
Σλ and Dλ
The two halves. Σ is a graded dimension: its coefficients are non-negative and it can never cancel. D is an index: differences of multiplicities, so it can vanish. Even windings carry Σ, odd windings carry D.
η = η₀η₁
The boundary-condition sign a multiplet carries, ±1. It multiplies the coset half and nothing else — which is why some multiplets cannot feel it at all.
blind
Dλ ≡ 0. The boundary condition has no consequence any measurement of this Higgs sector can see. Classified and counted in closed form, machine-checked in Lean 4.
box (p,q,r) and ζ
Part IV's closed form: Dλ = ζ χpχqχr, the three indices read off the 2-quotient of the partition. This is what makes the potential computable without enumerating a Kaluza–Klein spectrum.
matter / gauge
How the multiplet enters the loop: matter with a plus sign, the gauge and ghost sector with a minus and with the antiperiodic twist that exchanges the two mode counts.
Aq, Bq
Mode counts at charge q of each orbifold parity. A+B = Σ and A−B = D; the field writes the same pair as {A + B(−1)^k₂}.

What each label means. theorem proved, and machine-checked in Lean 4: which multiplets are blind to η, the count of them, and the census of boundary-condition classes. verified Part IV's closed form for Dλ, on which the mode counts rest — exhaustively checked, not proved. measured the vacuum, the masses and the mixing: the engine reproduces AHMN's published vacuum exactly and their published mass matrix to 0.1 %, and that single published case is the whole of its validation.

What this does not do. It says what a model predicts, not what nature does — in this model the Higgs comes out light, as AHMN themselves report. A closed form for the potential is not a closed form for the mass: the mass lives at the true minimum, found here numerically. The sign of the off-diagonal mass, and hence of the mixing angle, is a convention — the potential is invariant under α → −α, so only invariants are reported. Everything assumes boundary conditions that can be simultaneously diagonalised.
